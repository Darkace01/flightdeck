## New

- **Commit graph with branch lines.** See your history the way `git log --graph` draws it: branch lanes, merges, and branch, remote, tag and HEAD labels.
  - With a linked clone, **Commit graph** shows every branch. Type to highlight matching commits, and click one to see its message, parents, changed files and diffs.
  - From a commit you can **Branch here**, **Tag**, **Cherry-pick** it onto the current branch, **Revert** it, or **Reset** the current branch to it (soft, mixed or hard, with a confirmation).
  - Without a clone, **Commits** draws the selected branch's lines. **Branches** has a new **Graph** view of the default branch and the most recently updated branches.
- **GitLens-style history tools.** New **Local** sections in every repo tab:
  - **File history & blame:** every commit that changed a file (following renames), and who last changed each line, with a heat bar that highlights recent changes.
  - **Compare:** any two branches, remote branches or tags, with the commits only in each and every changed file's diff.
  - **Search commits:** across all branches, by message, author, SHA, file, or code that was added or removed.
  - **Stashes:** apply, pop or drop, with each stash's diff.
  - **Tags:** push or delete.
  - **Remotes & worktrees:** fetch from any remote; add a worktree to check out another branch in its own folder, or remove one.
  - **Contributors:** who has committed to the current branch, and how much.
- **Merge, rebase and delete local branches** from **Working copy → Branches**. You're warned before deleting a branch with unmerged work.
- **Vertical tabs.** Put your tabs down the side instead of across the top: **Settings → Appearance → Tabs**, or the tabs icon at the end of the tab bar.
- **Pinned repos.** Right-click any tab and choose **Pin tab**, or drag it onto the pinned area. Pinned repos sit at the top of the sidebar as tiles, each with its own colour, its owner's avatar and a CI-status dot. They stay open and come first in the tab order.
- **Repo sidebar.** Press `Ctrl/⌘+B`, or use the button at the top left, to show a sidebar with every repo you watch, one click away.
- **Tab menu.** Right-click a tab to pin or unpin it, close it or every other tab, watch or stop watching the repo, open it in the browser or copy its name.

## Improved

- **The Repositories widget fits its tiles.** It grows to show up to three rows of repos before scrolling, so you no longer have to resize it by hand.
- **Working copy → History** has moved into the new **Commit graph**, which also shows other branches.
