## New

- **Favourite repos.** Star a repo in Settings → Repositories to list it first everywhere and pin its tab.

## Improved

- **The first import watches at most 10 repos.** "Find repos on this computer" and importing a settings file into a fresh install now watch up to 10 repos to start, favourites first. Everything you picked is still linked, and you can add more afterwards.

- **Pushback polls less often once you watch 10 or more repos,** so a big list stays within GitHub's rate limit. The wait grows with the list: twice as long at 10 repos, three times at 20, up to five times.

- **Filters in repo tabs start collapsed.** Open them with the Filters button, like on dashboard widgets. They open by themselves if a filter is already on.

- **The top bar is now the window's title bar.** The empty system title bar above Pushback is gone: the bar with your dashboard tabs, search and buttons sits at the very top of the window. Drag any empty part of it to move the window, and double-click it to maximise. On Windows and Linux the minimise, maximise and close buttons sit at its right end in your theme's colours, and follow theme changes; on macOS the window buttons sit at its left end.

- **The top bar shows your GraphQL allowance next to the REST one.** Pull requests, the inbox and deployments use GitHub's GraphQL API, which has its own hourly limit. It turns red when less than a tenth is left.

## Fixed

- **Pushback no longer uses up GitHub's GraphQL limit.** While a workflow was running, pull requests and the inbox were fetched every 10 seconds for every repo, which could use up the hourly allowance in under an hour and leave the deployments view failing with "API rate limit already exceeded". Runs still refresh every 10 seconds while active; pull requests and the inbox now refresh at your normal refresh interval (or straight away when you press Refresh), and deployments every 3 minutes. If the limit is ever used up, Pushback stops asking until it resets, says when that will be, and shows one message instead of one per repo.

- **Deployments no longer fail with "Cannot read properties of null (reading 'refs')".** When GitHub can't find a tracked repo (it was renamed or deleted, or your token can't see it), the deployments view now says so in plain words for that repo, and the other repos keep loading.
